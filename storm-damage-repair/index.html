Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often

Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
